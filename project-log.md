## Week 1

**Completed:**

- Created the personal MTEC 3501 repository structure.
- Reviewed the Week 1 course setup and speculative-reflection assignment.
- Defined Clamorous E.L.F. as the provisional working project.
- Established the initial North Star Vision, potential Least Viable Product, and a narrower Proof of Concept direction.
- Confirmed that an audiovisual feedback loop requires live listening and watching rather than output alone.

**What changed in my thinking:**

The project is shifting toward a technically testable interaction loop. The initial system should not begin by implementing every proposed input and environmental component. Instead, the first version should prove that live audio or video input can produce an interpretable, adaptive audiovisual response.

**Discovery:**

The detailed proposal establishes the project as an emergent co-creative system, not a passive generative tool. It distinguishes environmental awareness, AI collaboration, and layered audiovisual output, but the production scope is broader than a first prototype can reasonably support.

**Question or problem:**

- Which modality should lead the Proof of Concept: microphone audio, camera/video input, or a tightly coupled paired implementation?
- How much AI processing can run in real time within the available hardware and network constraints?
- What constitutes a clear response to participant input versus random generative behavior?

**Next steps:**

- Choose the smallest modality that can be tested end to end.
- Record expected inputs, outputs, latency, and success criteria for the Proof of Concept.
- Begin exploratory technical and precedent research.
- Create an issue backlog after the initial prototype question is more clearly defined.
